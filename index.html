Traffic signals

Waiting for GPS.

Signal positions come from OpenStreetMap. If a signal is missing, press "Mark signal here" while stopped at it; the phone remembers it. "Everywhere" checks all the time and gives more false alarms.

Settings

Driver aid only. It can miss or misread lights and limits. Always watch the road yourself and do not touch the phone while driving.
