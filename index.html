Speed limit

Auto takes the limit from TomTom's map by GPS, and from the roadside boards read by the camera where the map has no answer. Tap a number to set it yourself.

TomTom key

With a TomTom key the limit comes from TomTom's navigation map, which is usually more complete than OpenStreetMap. A key is already built in. To change it, tap Edit, put in the new key, then tap Update; the new key is saved on this phone.

Traffic signals

Waiting for GPS.

Signal positions come from OpenStreetMap. Near a known signal the alarm comes after 2 sightings of red. Elsewhere the camera still watches and the alarm needs 3 sightings; a signal found this way is saved on this phone. "Mark signal here" saves one yourself while stopped at it. "Everywhere" uses 2 sightings all the time and gives more false alarms.

Settings

Normal is the tested setting. High gives a sharper picture but has more to search, and on the road it found signals later.
